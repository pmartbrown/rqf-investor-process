Your capital is protected at every step.

How Real Quick Funds funds Stack and Morby deals. We verify the title company, verify every wire, control the account the funds move through, fund last, and get repaid at a second closing right after the first.

The Process

01

Verify the title company

Before any money moves, we check the title company's domain age, its Google reviews and history, and its Secretary of State registration, including whether the registered address matches its real place of business. On larger deals, we also confirm it carries enough E&O insurance.

02

Secure the account

Funds go through a special-purpose account for the purchasing LLC, and we take full control of the account to ensure security for any funds we deposit.

03

Verify the wire

We call the escrow agent to confirm banking details before sending anything. We never use the number printed on wire instructions, only the one on the title company's own website or Google listing.

04

Fund last

We're generally the last party to fund, and only after buyer and seller have signed and executed all documents. The one exception: if the primary lender requires our deposit first, we fund just ahead of them.

05

Close the first leg

Funds are wired to the title company, and escrow instructions direct them, at closing, into a separate escrow.

06

Second closing & repayment

The second-position note (Stack or Morby) or the preferred-equity paperwork is recorded. Our principal and fees are paid out at that second closing, immediately after the first.
